---
title: New years day in Paris
location: Paris
---

We woke up to a very, very quiet city. Despite of the security threats, Paris went hard. Most things were closed, but we managed to get a good cup of coffee from [Bonjour Jacob](https://www.bonjourjacob.com/) before wandering along the Seine towards the Eiffel tower. The lines for the [Musée D'Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/en) and [Le Grand Palais](https://www.grandpalais.fr) were way too long, so after walking around we got another coffee from [Parallel Coffee](https://www.instagram.com/parallel.coffee/?hl=en). Sufficiently warmed, we took a quick river tour on the Seine, then got dinner at [L'Avant Comptoir de la Mer](https://camdeborde.com/les-restaurants/avant-comptoir-de-la-mer).
