title: Bumming around Bordeaux
location: Bordeaux
---

We woke up around 8, but then lazed in bed. Got coffee from [Kuro Espresso Bar](https://kuroespressobar.wixsite.com/website), before wandering around and getting lunch at [WOODKITCHEN](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187079-d10641216-Reviews-Wood_Kitchen-Bordeaux_Gironde_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html). Payom had the lasagne bolognese, while Katie and I split the lasagna with goat cheap and spinach and a Caesar. [L’alchemist](https://www.alchimiste-cafes.com), the coffee shop where we serendipitously ran into Guillaume’s mom and her class the last time we were in Bordeaux was literally around the corner, so we got coffee there before wandering around a bit more. Payom found some art he liked, we took another gander at the church, then headed back for a late afternoon nap.

We got a super tasty dinner at [Papin](https://www.instagram.com/papin_tableedequartier_bdx/), though Payom was feeling off and left early. The flies LOVED Katie’s black cod, so we all spent half our meal waving away the flies. Post, we got gelato from [Gelato & Basta](https://www.instagram.com/gelatoetbasta/), then headed to bed.
