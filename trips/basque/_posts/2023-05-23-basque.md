Walked around San Sebastián. Walked up to [La Castillo de la Mota](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castillo_de_la_Mota_(San_Sebasti%C3%A1n)) and reread our vows to each other.

Saw some great views of the city, then on the way back down came across a random sign that read "Bar". So, we stopped and sat looking out over the city. I had a [Zumo](https://basquebeer.com/en/zumo-hazy-ipa-can-440ml-4-pack/), which was definitely the best beer of the trip. Katie had another glass of Txakoli.

For dinner we went to [Kokotxa](https://restaurantekokotxa.com/en/homepage/). They initially lost our reservation and service was a little shakey to start, but once the meal really started, it evened out. The food was great!
