We woke up and took the HSR to Taipei. we dropped our bags at the [Diary of Ximen](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Diary+of+Ximen+Hotel+Taipei) hotel, and then went exploring. First, we headed to the [Wistaria tea house](https://www.wistariateahouse.com/) where we passed a lovely hour or two sitting around, drinking tea, and chatting. From there, we wandered around Da’an, walked through the [Da’an Forest Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daan_Forest_Park), unsuccessfully tried to figure out what a pergola is, and then headed backstop the hotel.

Refreshed, we checked out the [Ximen night market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ximending), then headed to the [Raohe night market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raohe_Street_Night_Market). It was crazy, shoulder to shoulder the entire night. Really cool spectacle though, and excellent food to match. Amy even ate a passion fruit! We got back to the hotel late, maybe around midnight, and then crashed.
